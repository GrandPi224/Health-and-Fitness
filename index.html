NOC to Stecoah Gap

13.5 miles. Start at the Nantahala Outdoor Center, climb west for 8 miles to Cheoah Bald, then head north for 5.5 miles, mostly downhill, to Stecoah Gap on NC 143. This is the section you missed when your knee swelled.

Route map

North is up. Green S is the start, gold F is the finish. Numbered circles are mile markers. The arrows show your direction: left, west, to Cheoah Bald, then up, north, to Stecoah Gap.

Elevation profile

The green band is the main climb. The orange band is the steepest descent, the hardest stretch for your knee. Go slow there and lean on your poles.

Knee plan. Trekking poles, knee brace or sleeve, anti-inflammatories, elastic wrap. Decide at Sassafras Gap Shelter, mile 6.8: if the knee is sore, turn back there. No road crosses the trail between the start and the finish.
